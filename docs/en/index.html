Get started

Install in a minute

  1. Download the normal APK. No GitHub account is required. Open the file and allow installation from that browser or file manager if Android asks. Revoke this permission after installation if you prefer.
  2. Connect your headphones. Pair them through Android's Bluetooth settings.
  3. Open AirMode. Allow Nearby devices / Bluetooth. Notifications are optional. Tap Done.
  4. Add quick access. AirMode Settings → Add home-screen widget or Add Quick Settings tile. You can also use the launcher's widget list or edit the expanded shade.
Keep settings when updating. Install the next official APK over the previous version. Use the diagnostics APK only for troubleshooting. The published app retains its signing identity.

Devices and versions

Modern Android.
Clear compatibility.

AirMode installs on Android 12+, including Android 16 and 17. AirPods noise control requires an accessible Bluetooth channel; some phone firmware may provide battery only. Physical stem/button controls work without AirMode.

Verified results

A user reports good AirPods operation and Sony battery display. Physical reports from Pixel 10 Pro · Android 17 · CP41.260831.007.A3 · AirPods 5 A3532 include left/right battery and replies for all four modes. The Sony model is unspecified; Sony noise switching has not been separately confirmed.

The official APK also installs and starts in Android 16 and Android 17 beta / API 37 / 16 KB emulators. Pixel 11 Pro, other firmware and the latest QPR3 beta DP11.260918.005/.006 still need separate hardware evidence. Future betas can change the Bluetooth stack.

Help

Answers within reach

Why is the case battery unknown?

Open the case near your phone and tap Refresh battery. Short scan windows are at least 15 seconds apart. A closed or distant case may report nothing. Older readings are labelled; unknown charge is never replaced with zero or 100%.

Why are noise controls unavailable?

The model may lack ANC, still be unidentified, or Android may block its control channel. For AirPods, try Pixel Android 16 QPR3 / Android 17. For Sony, close Sound Connect and reconnect. An unknown firmware may provide only battery.

How do the tile and widget work?

The widget selects a mode directly. The tile cycles modes selected in Settings; long-press it to open AirMode. Selection follows headphone confirmation. Controls are unavailable when disconnected.

Why does the connection popup not appear?

Enable connection notifications in AirMode and allow them in Android. Check the Battery channel and Do Not Disturb. Android decides whether to show heads-up. Notification refusal does not block battery readings in the app.

Is it private and free?

The app has no INTERNET, location or microphone permission, account, ads or analytics. Headphone data stays on your phone. Downloads and external links use your browser. Code is Apache-2.0; proximity keys stay only in session memory.

How can I report a problem?

Open an Issue with AirMode version, phone, Android Build ID, headphone model/firmware and reproduction steps. Do not publish Bluetooth addresses, serial numbers or keys. A safe diagnostic APK and instructions are in the README.
